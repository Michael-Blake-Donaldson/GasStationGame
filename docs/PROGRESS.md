## Honest capability boundary

The current build has a real deterministic day-business loop: routine customers queue for attributed pump/checkout work, employee skill and fatigue change exact service speed/rework risk, staffing changes requeue interrupted customers, inventory and price commands affect causal sales, and the shift board exposes every modifier. Construction spends exact resources, creates deterministic saved/replayed occupancy, protects current employees and remaining active routes, and preserves a required employee/work-area network before rendering generic authoritative shells. Those shells do not yet provide power, defense, openable gates, storage, repair, or utility behavior; customer/vehicle, threat/defender, evacuation, and utility routes remain with their owning systems. The broader screen remains a presentation/architecture scaffold: employee movement is not rendered; recovery status and manual save/load are not player-facing; browser development is intentionally ephemeral; skill/fatigue do not yet progress or recover. The repository does **not** yet implement automatic combat, actual power allocation, traveler dialogue, the signature creature, wind/fire, audio, or the complete three-night scenario.
